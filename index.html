Excel & Power BI

Superstore Sales
Analysis

Analysed Superstore sales data to assess sales, profit, customer, product, regional, and operational performance. The four-page dashboard identifies how discounting, returns, product mix, and customer value affect profitability.

Tools: Microsoft Excel, Power BI, Power Query, PowerPoint.
Selected insight: Sales reached $2.30M with $286.40K profit, while high-selling tables recorded a loss—highlighting the need to evaluate revenue alongside profitability.
